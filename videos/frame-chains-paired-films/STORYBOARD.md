## Video direction

- **Palette:** use `frame.md` exactly: white paper/tile fields, `#242424` ink, one `#0067b8` voltage moment per scene, and warm navy only for technical proof surfaces. The source footage keeps its native colors inside the editorial frame.
- **Type:** Segoe UI display and body; Consolas for proof labels, frame indices, hashes, and verdict chrome. Display remains sentence case.
- **Motion grammar:** smooth long-tail arrivals, never bounce. Reveal each proof layer when the narration names it, with most development in the back half. Use explicit crop-wrapper camera poses on footage, not drifting timed clips.
- **Footage treatment:** preserve UI color and text fidelity. Use clean crop/reframe, a hairline editorial window, selective depth-of-field on non-focal surrounding layers, and restrained contrast framing rather than stylized pixel effects.
- **Rhythm:** Frames 1, 5, and 9 carry the largest motion turns. Frames 4 and 7 are comparative held reads. Frame 10 settles into the longest final invitation.
- **Caption keep-out:** all proof text, verdicts, and controls stay inside the top 83% of the frame.
- **Never:** tiny full-page screen recordings, browser chrome, generic device mockups, decorative fake dashboards, lazy breathing, back-half camera drift, independent floating elements, bouncy easing, or front-loaded slideshow reveals.
