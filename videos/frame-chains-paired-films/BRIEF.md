## Intent

Produce a real encoded-video suite for the five existing Frame Chains RAPP Vision
entries. The flagship is a 75-90 second narrated film, **Ten Impossible Worlds,
One Verifiable History**, built from actual browser footage of all ten public
showcase applications. Four focused 45-60 second films cover Many Worlds, AI
Soul Passport, Teleporting Roguelike, and Attack the Timeline.

The video is the newcomer orientation layer: it explains what the viewer is
seeing, why the result matters, and which success or rejection to watch for.
The existing scripted replay is the take-the-wheel proof. Both belong to the
same RAPP Vision publication; neither format is complete by itself.

## Customizations

- Capture and feature the applications' real rendered screens rather than rebuilding them as decorative mockups.
- Use close crops and editorial callouts so proof states remain readable at normal playback size.
- Add English narration, styled captions, a restrained cinematic technology score, and proof-state sound accents.
- Deliver MP4 and WebM for each film.
- Preserve each existing scripted live replay beside its encoded film.
- Keep the flagship to exactly ten main proof scenes, one per public frame.

## Notes

- Public synthetic material only.
- No desktop capture, local paths, repository inventory, private IP addresses, credentials, prompts, PII, or proprietary estate data.
- Do not produce a generic tiny-UI screen-recording montage.
- The opening must explain the paired model in plain language: watch the film to understand it, then open the live replay to operate it.
- The most typical direction deliberately rejected is a silent product tour that assumes the viewer already understands Frame Chains.
- Remembered user defaults applied: 1920x1080, English, automation, and no storyboard checkpoint.
