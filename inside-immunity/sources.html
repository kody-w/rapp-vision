T Cells

An original five-minute introduction to recognition, teamwork, restraint, and memory.

All biological diagrams are simplified and not to scale. Foundational textbook references are used for stable mechanisms, not current treatment approvals. The medical examples are educational, not personal advice.

Scientific references

R1. NIAID - Immune System Overview

Bone marrow origins; maturation in the upper-chest thymus; lymphatic meeting sites.

https://www.niaid.nih.gov/research/immune-system-overview

R2. NIAID - Immune Cells

T/B roles, CD4/CD8 recognition, MHC presentation, cytokines, apoptosis, and regulation.

https://www.niaid.nih.gov/research/immune-cells

R3. Immunobiology - Selection of lymphocytes

Peptide-MHC recognition and positive/negative thymic selection; foundational 2001 reference.

https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK27089/

R4. NIAID - Immune Tolerance

Autoreactive cells can remain despite developmental selection; additional tolerance mechanisms.

https://www.niaid.nih.gov/research/immune-tolerance

R5. Immunobiology - The course of the adaptive response

Dendritic-cell migration, innate context, co-stimulation, and selective expansion.

https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK27125/

R6. Immunobiology - Immunological memory

Response contraction, persistent memory, faster recall, and B-derived plasma cells.

https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK27158/

R7. NIH - Immune response improves after vaccination

A specific vaccination example engaging T and B cells; not a universal duration estimate.

https://www.nih.gov/news-events/nih-research-matters/covid-19-immune-response-improves-months-after-vaccination

R8. NIH - Vaccine immune responses to Omicron

Cellular responses can remain relevant despite breakthrough infection; no infection-proof claim.

https://www.nih.gov/news-events/nih-research-matters/covid-19-vaccines-induce-immune-response-omicron

R9. NIH HIVinfo - HIV and AIDS: The Basics

HIV attacks CD4 cells and compromises immune defenses.

https://hivinfo.nih.gov/understanding-hiv/fact-sheets/hiv-and-aids-basics

R10. NCI - Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors

Checkpoint restraint, therapeutic blockade, and possible inflammatory side effects.

https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types/immunotherapy/checkpoint-inhibitors

R11. NCI - T-cell Transfer Therapy

Engineered receptors can bind selected surface proteins; restricted applications and serious risks.

https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types/immunotherapy/t-cell-transfer-therapy

R12. NCI - Immunotherapy to Treat Cancer

Patient responses vary, resistance exists, and healthy tissues can be affected.

https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types/immunotherapy

R13. Immunobiology - Vaccination

Protective requirements and T-cell involvement vary among pathogens and vaccines.

https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK27131/
